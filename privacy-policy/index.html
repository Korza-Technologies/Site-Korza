Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Last updated: December 18th 2025

This Privacy Policy and embedded Data Processing Addendum ("Policy") describes how Korza Inc. ("Korza," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and processes personal data in connection with its merchant-facing SaaS platform, including the web dashboard and ecommerce integrations.

This document also incorporates a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) governing Korza's role as a data processor under applicable data protection laws, including the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD).

1. Company Information

Legal name: Korza Inc.
Incorporation: United States
Website: https://korza.app
Contact email: support@korza.app

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies exclusively to merchants and business users who use Korza's:

  • Web dashboard
  • APIs and integrations with ecommerce platforms (e.g. Shopify)

This Policy does not apply to end users (shoppers) who interact with merchant mobile applications built using Korza. The privacy policy for each app can be found in its respective Google Play and App Store pages.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

Depending on the context, Korza acts as:

Data Controller for:

  • Merchant account information
  • Platform usage analytics
  • Billing-related metadata

Data Processor (Operador) for:

  • Ecommerce data (specifically order data) accessed via third-party integrations, processed strictly on behalf of merchants

Merchants act as Data Controllers (Controladores) for all data originating from their ecommerce platforms.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Merchant Account Data

We collect:

  • Merchant email address
  • Account identifiers
  • Authentication credentials
  • Support communications

4.2 Billing Data

Billing and payments are handled by third-party providers such as:

  • Stripe
  • Shopify Billing / Checkout

Korza does not store or process full payment card information.

4.3 Ecommerce Integration Data

When authorized by the merchant, Korza may access:

  • Order data only

Korza does not access customer payment details and does not access customer personal data where the ecommerce platform restricts such access (e.g. Shopify).

4.4 Usage and Technical Data

We may collect:

  • Dashboard usage analytics
  • Log files and operational metrics
  • IP address and device/browser information

5. How We Use Data

Korza processes merchant data to:

  • Provide and operate the SaaS platform
  • Authenticate and manage merchant accounts
  • Display analytics derived from order data
  • Process subscriptions and billing
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve security, reliability, and performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

Korza does not send marketing emails to merchants.

6. Push Notifications

Korza provides technical infrastructure that allows merchants to send marketing and transactional push notifications to their own customers via merchant-controlled mobile applications.

Korza does not determine notification content, recipients, or legal basis for such communications.

7. Cookies

The Korza web dashboard uses essential cookies only, required for:

  • Authentication
  • Security
  • Core platform functionality

No marketing or tracking cookies are used.

8. Data Storage and Security

8.1 Hosting

Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the us-east-2 region.

8.2 Security Measures

Korza implements appropriate safeguards, including:

  • Encryption of data at rest and in transit
  • Access controls and least-privilege policies
  • Monitoring and logging

9. Data Retention

  • Merchant data is retained for the duration of the active account.
  • After account cancellation, data is retained for up to 30 days, after which it is deleted unless legal retention is required.

10. Merchant Rights (LGPD)

In accordance with the LGPD, merchants may:

  • Request access to personal data
  • Request correction or updating
  • Request data export
  • Request deletion, subject to legal obligations

Requests may be made via privacy@korza.app.

12. Shopify Compliance

Korza complies with the Shopify Partner Program and processes Shopify data strictly within the permissions granted by merchants and Shopify's applicable terms.

13. Changes to This Policy

Korza may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the dashboard or by email.
